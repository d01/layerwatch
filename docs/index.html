Layerwatch

Watch and control your Bambu Lab 3D printer from Android, directly over your own network. No cloud account, no ads, no tracking. Free and open source.

  • Live chamber camera, built to ride out weak printer WiFi
  • Progress, temperatures, AMS spools and drying, alerts in plain English
  • Alerts with the phone locked, and a Live Update while a print runs
  • 3D build view, finish-time forecast, filament check, timelapses
  • Pause, stop and speed behind press-and-hold safety catches
